# PM-KISAN — Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi

## Overview
PM-KISAN is a central government scheme that provides direct income support of ₹6,000 per year to all landholding farmer families across India. The amount is paid in three equal installments of ₹2,000 each, directly into the farmer's Aadhaar-linked bank account.

## Eligibility
- All landholding farmer families are eligible, regardless of the size of their landholding.
- The scheme covers Small and Marginal Farmers (SMFs) as well as all other farmer categories.
- Both husband and wife can receive benefits if both are separately registered farmers with distinct land records.

## Who is NOT Eligible
- Institutional landholders (companies, trusts)
- Former and present holders of constitutional posts
- Former and present Ministers, MPs, MLAs
- All serving or retired officers and employees of Central/State Government
- All persons paying income tax in the last assessment year
- Professionals like Doctors, Engineers, Lawyers, Chartered Accountants registered with professional bodies

## How to Apply
1. Visit the nearest Common Service Centre (CSC) or Raitha Samparka Kendra
2. Carry Aadhaar card, bank passbook, and land records (RTC/Pahani)
3. The CSC operator will register you on the PM-KISAN portal
4. You can also self-register at pmkisan.gov.in
5. Complete e-KYC using Aadhaar OTP or biometric verification

## Important Dates
- Installments are released in April-July, August-November, and December-March periods
- e-KYC must be completed to receive installments
- Land records (RTC) must be updated and linked

## Karnataka-Specific Information
- Karnataka farmers can apply through Raitha Samparka Kendra or Grama One centres
- Land records are verified through Bhoomi portal (land.karnataka.gov.in)
- For help, contact the Agriculture Department helpline: 1800-180-1551
- District Agriculture Officers can assist with registration issues

## Amount Details
- ₹2,000 per installment × 3 installments = ₹6,000 per year
- Money is transferred directly to the bank account
- No middlemen or intermediaries involved
- Farmers can check payment status at pmkisan.gov.in using Aadhaar or mobile number
