# KCC — Kisan Credit Card

## Overview
The Kisan Credit Card (KCC) scheme provides affordable institutional credit to farmers for agricultural activities, post-harvest expenses, and consumption requirements. It was launched to ensure timely and adequate credit to farmers at reduced interest rates.

## Key Features
- Credit limit based on the farmer's landholding, cropping pattern, and scale of finance
- Interest rate: 7% per annum, with a subvention of 3% for prompt repayment, making the effective rate just 4%
- Flexible repayment tied to harvesting season
- One-time documentation with a validity of 5 years (with annual renewal)
- Personal accident insurance cover of ₹50,000 for death and ₹25,000 for permanent disability
- Can be used for crop production, post-harvest, maintenance of farm assets, consumption, and investment

## Eligibility
- All farmers — owner cultivators, tenant farmers, sharecroppers
- Self Help Groups (SHGs) and Joint Liability Groups (JLGs) of farmers
- Fishermen and animal husbandry farmers are also eligible under revised KCC

## How to Apply
1. Visit any commercial bank, cooperative bank, or Regional Rural Bank
2. Carry Aadhaar card, PAN card, land records (RTC/Pahani), and 2 passport photos
3. Fill the KCC application form
4. The bank will assess your credit limit based on land size and crop
5. KCC card will be issued within 14 working days

## Credit Limit Calculation
- Crop Loan component: Based on area cultivated, scale of finance, and cropping pattern
- Post-harvest/household needs: 10% of crop loan
- Maintenance of farm assets: 20% of crop loan limit
- The limit increases by 10% every year for inflation

## Important Notes
- Prompt repayment gives 3% interest subvention (effective rate = 4%)
- Late payment attracts standard 7% interest
- The KCC can also be linked to Aadhaar for Direct Benefit Transfer
- ATM-enabled RuPay debit card is issued for easy access
