# PMFBY — Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (Crop Insurance)

## Overview
PMFBY is a comprehensive crop insurance scheme that protects farmers against crop loss due to natural calamities, pests, and diseases. It covers the entire crop cycle from pre-sowing to post-harvest, including prevented sowing and mid-season adversities.

## Premium Rates
- Kharif crops (paddy, ragi, maize, groundnut, etc.): 2% of sum insured
- Rabi crops (wheat, gram, mustard, etc.): 1.5% of sum insured
- Commercial and horticultural crops (cotton, sugarcane, vegetables): 5% of sum insured
- The remaining premium is shared between Central and State governments

## Coverage
- Prevented Sowing: If sowing cannot be done due to deficit rainfall or adverse weather, up to 25% of the sum insured is payable
- Standing Crop Damage: Covers yield losses due to non-preventable risks like drought, flood, hailstorm, cyclone, pest/disease attack
- Post-Harvest Losses: Covers crop loss up to 14 days after harvest for crops spread in the field for drying
- Localized Calamities: Covers hailstorm, landslide, and inundation affecting individual farms
- Add-on coverage for wild animal attack available in some areas

## How to Enroll
1. Loanee farmers: Automatically enrolled through their bank (can opt out if not interested)
2. Non-loanee farmers: Apply through CSC, bank branch, or PMFBY app/website
3. Documents needed: Aadhaar, bank account, land records (RTC), sowing certificate
4. Enrollment deadline: Before the cutoff date announced each season

## Claim Process
1. Report crop loss within 72 hours of the calamity
2. Call the toll-free number 1800-200-7710 or use the Crop Insurance App
3. Or report to the bank, agriculture office, or insurance company
4. Crop Cutting Experiments (CCE) determine the yield in the area
5. If the actual yield is less than the threshold yield, the difference is compensated

## Karnataka-Specific Details
- Karnataka has notified PMFBY for major Kharif and Rabi crops
- Enrollment through Raitha Samparka Kendra and cooperative banks
- Karnataka State Natural Disaster Monitoring Centre (KSNDMC) provides weather data for claims
- Helpline for Karnataka: Contact District Agriculture Office
